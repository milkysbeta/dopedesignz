Made after you order it

Pick the art, then the garment

Every design is shown on the actual AS Colour blank and colour you are buying. Change either one and the mockup redraws — no guessing what the print looks like on pine green.

We send it to Print Poppa

Your order goes straight to their Auckland studio with the artwork, garment code, colour and size attached. Nothing is printed until it is sold.

Direct-to-garment printed, quality checked and posted from Tāmaki Makaurau. Three to five working days New Zealand wide.
