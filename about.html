About

Art that was moving before it was worn

Everything here starts as something running — a shader, a wave field, a system left to iterate until it produces a frame worth keeping. The hard part is choosing the frame. What you end up wearing is one still from something that never actually stops.

Printed to order, here

Every piece is printed by Print Poppa in Tāmaki Makaurau after you order it — direct-to-garment on cotton, heat transfer on the poly shells. Nothing is printed in advance, so there is no dead stock, no end-of-season landfill, and no size that sells out because someone guessed wrong in a spreadsheet.

It also means the range can be genuinely large. A design can exist on four garments in six colours without anyone committing to a single unit of inventory.

Artists get paid on sales

Guest artists are not bought out. Their name stays on the work through the shop, the cart, and the print docket that goes to the studio, and they earn on each piece sold rather than once for the file.

If a design stops selling it comes out of rotation rather than getting discounted into oblivion.

Orders are printed within 1–3 working days and posted from Auckland. Tracking comes through by email as soon as the label is made.

Because each piece is made for you specifically, we cannot take change-of-mind returns. Anything faulty, misprinted or damaged in transit is replaced or refunded in full — email within 30 days with a photo and it is sorted. If a garment does not fit, get in touch anyway; we would rather find a way than have it sit in a drawer.

Fit

Sizing

Full measurement tables live on each product page under Size guide — they are per garment, because a hoodie and a coach jacket do not share a chest measurement. Everything is measured flat in centimetres with a 1–2cm tolerance. The tees run true to size with a boxy shoulder; size up if you want the print to sit lower and the body to drape.

Questions about an order? Email hello@example.co.nz with your order reference and it gets answered by an actual person, usually the same day.
